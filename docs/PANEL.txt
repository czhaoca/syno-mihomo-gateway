Gateway Panel
=============

The gateway panel is the stack's third container. It gives every LAN device
a per-device routing mode you can change at any time - without editing .env
and without restarting anything:

      full-tunnel   ALL of the device's traffic rides the proxy
      full-direct   NONE of it does (always direct)
      default       the normal rule-based routing

Open the panel from any LAN browser:

      http://<PANEL_IP>:8090/ui/

(<PANEL_IP> is the address you chose during install; the port is PANEL_PORT
in .env if you changed it.) The page also shows per-device traffic history.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Changing a device's mode
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
In the UI: find the device row (or add its IP), pick the mode, confirm.
The change is live within a second or two - the panel tells mihomo to
reload just the two dynamic rule files.

From the command line on the NAS:

      sh scripts/gateway.sh policy --list
      sh scripts/gateway.sh policy --set 192.168.1.50 --mode full-tunnel
      sh scripts/gateway.sh policy --set 192.168.1.50 --mode default

"default" removes the entry. The command prints the panel's honest answer:
"applied": true means the change reached mihomo; if it says false, the
change is SAVED but not live yet - run the doctor (below).

Devices listed in FULL_PROXY_SOURCES (.env) show a "band" badge in the UI.
A panel entry OVERRIDES the band for that address; the band itself only
changes at deploy time.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
LAN destinations stay direct
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
A full-tunnel device still reaches the NAS, the router, and everything else
on your LAN directly - only its internet traffic rides the proxy.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Passwords and safety
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every CHANGE needs the panel token (PANEL_SECRET in .env - the installer
generated it for you). READING the page is open to your LAN.

NEVER expose the panel to the internet - no port-forward, no tunnel, no
cloudflared entry pointing at it. Even with a strong token, anyone on the
internet could READ your device list and traffic history; the token only
protects changes.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
If something looks wrong
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Run the doctor:

      sudo sh scripts/doctor.sh

- "companion_health" warns when the panel container is down or degraded.
- "policy_parity" reports an ERROR when what the panel saved and what
  mihomo routes have drifted apart. Fix: open the UI (it re-applies on a
  click of Apply), or restart the panel container:

      sudo docker restart mihomo-panel

Your policy entries and statistics live in the data directory under
state/panel/ and survive restarts, updates, and redeploys. See
TROUBLESHOOTING.txt for the full failure walkthrough.
